# WeightMe Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 7, 2026_

WeightMe is a weight tracker built on Apple Health. It is designed so that your data never leaves your
control.

## What WeightMe does with your data

- **Apple Health.** With your permission, WeightMe reads and writes one kind of Health data: **Weight**
  (body mass). It reads your weight history to show charts and statistics, and writes the weights you
  log or import. You can change or revoke this access at any time in the Settings app › Health › Data
  Access & Devices › WeightMe.
- **On your device only.** Weights are stored in Apple Health. WeightMe also keeps a copy on your device
  (in the app's private storage and, for the widget, a small summary in the app's shared container) so it
  opens quickly. Deleting the app deletes these copies; your Apple Health data stays in Apple Health.
- **CSV files.** WeightMe reads a CSV file only when you choose one to import, and creates one only when
  you export. Where an exported file goes (Files, AirDrop, email…) is up to you.

## What WeightMe does not do

- It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or health data. It has no servers.
- It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs, and requires no account.
- It does not use Apple Health data for advertising or any purpose other than showing it back to you.
